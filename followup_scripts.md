# 🔄 CGP360: Follow-up Scripts (Call & WhatsApp)

> **Mindset:** You are a **Clinic Growth Partner**, not a salesman. Your goal is to help the doctor see the value they are missing out on.

---

## 📅 Follow-up Schedule Overview
- **Day 2-3:** Stage 1 - The "Helpful Insight" (Checking the Demo)
- **Day 5-7:** Stage 2 - The "Trust & Proof" (Success Story / Ranking)
- **Day 10-12:** Stage 3 - The "Urgency/Limited Slot" (The Offer)
- **Day 15+:** Stage 4 - The "Break-up" (Closing the File)

---

## 🟢 Stage 1: The Helpful Insight (Day 2-3)
*Goal: Confirm they saw the demo and point out a specific "gap" in their current presence.*

### 📱 WhatsApp Message
"Greetings Dr. [Name] 🙏,

I hope you had a chance to look at the **Demos** I shared yesterday.

I have prepared a **'Growth Action Plan'** for your clinic. One key feature we'll be implementing is **'Instant Booking'**, which will allow patients to message your WhatsApp directly from the website.

Which design (Theme) did you find most professional?

Regards, **[Your Name]**"

### 📞 Call Script
**You:** "Namaste Doctor Sahab, **[Your Name]** baat kar raha hoon CGP360 se. Kaise hain aap?"
*(Wait for response)*
**You:** "Sir, maine 2 din pehle aapko kuch demo websites bheji thin. Kya aapko unhe dekhne ka mauka mila?"
**If NO:** "Koi baat nahi sir, main samajh sakta hoon clinic mein rush hota hai. Aaj raat ko ek baar zaroor check kijiye, khas karke wo **Dental/Skin [Specific Category]** wala demo. Wo aapke clinic ki dignity ko suite karega."
**If YES:** "Great! Sir, sabse achha feature aapko kaunsa laga? Wo WhatsApp booking button ya professional gallery?"

---

## 🔵 Stage 2: Trust & Proof (Day 5-7)
*Goal: Show them that others are growing and they are falling behind.*

### 📱 WhatsApp Message
"Greetings Doctor [Name] 🙏,

Just wanted to share a quick update. We recently launched a website for another clinic, and their Google Maps ranking has already moved to the **Top 3** in their area (Screenshot attached 📸).

Your clinic is one of the best in the locality, but without a strong digital presence, you are missing out on new patients.

With just a **₹2,400 (30%)** booking amount, we can start your setup tomorrow.

Shall we proceed?

Regards, **[Your Name]**"

### 📞 Call Script
**You:** "Namaste Doctor Sahab, **[Your Name]** baat kar raha hoon CGP360 se. Kaise hain aap?"
*(Wait for response)*
**You:** "Sir, maine aapko ek screenshot bheja hai WhatsApp par. Humne abhi ek clinic ka setup complete kiya aur wo area mein **Top 3** mein rank kar rahe hain Google par."
**You:** "Sir, aapka clinic itna purana aur reputed hai, par online presence na hone ki wajah se naye patients pados waale clinic ke paas chale jaate hain jinki website hai."
**You:** "Kya hum aapke clinic ka setup bhi shuru karein? Sirf **30% (₹2,400)** se start kar sakte hain hum."

---

## 🟡 Stage 3: The Urgency / Limited Slot (Day 10-12)
*Goal: Create a reason for them to act NOW.*

### 📱 WhatsApp Message
"Greetings Dr. [Name] 🙏,

I am just following up on your file. This month, we are only onboarding **12 Doctors** for specialized growth setups (including Free SEO & Maintenance), and only **2 slots** are remaining.

I would love to have your clinic included in this special one-time offer (₹8,000).

If we confirm today, we can have your professional website ready within 3 days.

Payment Link: `[Your Payment Link]`

Regards, **[Your Name]**"

### 📞 Call Script
**You:** "Namaste Doctor Sahab, **[Your Name]** baat kar raha hoon CGP360 se."
**You:** "Sir, actually is month hum sirf **12 clinics** ka setup kar rahe hain (Free SEO aur Maintenance ke sath), aur mere paas sirf **2 slots** bache hain."
**You:** "Maine socha ek baar aapse final confirm kar loon, kyunki aapke area se 2-3 enquiries aur aayi hui hain. Lekin main pehle aapko preference Dena chahta tha."
**You:** "Kya hum aapka slot book karein? Sirf **₹2,400** lagega start karne ke liye."

---

## 🔴 Stage 4: The Break-up (Day 15+)
*Goal: Take away the offer to trigger a response.*

### 📱 WhatsApp Message (Final)
"Greetings Dr. [Name] 🙏,

I assume you are currently busy with other projects or perhaps do not require a professional website at this moment.

I am marking your file as 'Closed' for now to avoid sending you further reminders.

If you require **Clinic Growth** support or a professional website in the future, feel free to reach out.

Have a great practice!

Regards, **[Your Name]**"

### 📞 Call Script (Optional Final Touch)
**You:** "Namaste Doctor Sahab, **[Your Name]** from CGP360."
**You:** "Sir, main bus ye batane ke liye call kiya tha ki hamara is month ka session close ho raha hai, toh main aapki file 'Closed' mark kar raha hoon."
**You:** "Future mein kabhi bhi aapko help chahiye ho clinic grow karne mein, toh mera number save rakhiyega. All the best for your practice, Sir!"

---

## 🛡️ Pro Tips for Follow-ups
1. **Timing:** Messages 11:00 AM - 1:00 PM ke beech bhejein (OPD time ke beech ka break).
2. **Personalization:** Hamesha Doctor ka naam use karein.
3. **Voice Notes:** Kabhi-kabhi WhatsApp par chota sa voice note (Hinglish mein) zyada trust build karta hai text se.
4. **Don't Spam:** Agar 4 stages ke baad reply nahi aata, toh stop. Focus on new leads.

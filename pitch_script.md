# 📞 CGP360: Ultimate Sales Pitch Script (Hinglish)

> **Goal:** Appointment Book karna ya WhatsApp par Demo bhejna.
> **Tone:** Professional, Respectful, Consultant (Not Salesman).

---

## 🟢 Phase 1: The Opening (Respect & Context)
*Don't start selling immediately. Build a tiny connection first.*

**You:** "Hello, Good Morning! Kya meri baat **Dr. [Name]** se ho rahi hai?"
*(Wait for Yes)*

**You:** "Namaste Doctor Sahab. Mera naam **[Your Name]** hai aur main **CGP360 - Clinic Growth Partners** se baat kar raha hoon. Actually Doctor, maine abhi Google Maps par aapka clinic dekha..."

*(Pause - let them wonder. This is a pattern interrupt.)*

**You:** "Reviews toh kaafi achhe hain aapke, lekin ek choti si cheez missing thi jo aapke patients dhoondh rahe honge. Kya main bus 1 minute le sakta hoon batane ke liye?"

*(If they say YES -> Proceed)*
*(If they say BUSY -> "Koi baat nahi sir, main aapko WhatsApp par detail bhej deta hoon, aap free hokar dekh lijiye." -> **Send WhatsApp**)*

---

## 🟠 Phase 2: The Problem (Dard)
*Highlight the 'Gap' between them and their competitor.*

**You:** "Sir, aajkal **lagbhag 80%** patients doctor ke paas aane se pehle Google par search karte hain. Aapka Google Map toh hai, par wahan **'Website'** ka button khaali hai."

**You:** "Jab naya patient dekhta hai ki clinic ki koi website nahi hai, toh unhe lagta hai ki clinic updated nahi hai ya professional kam hai. Aur wo wahi pados waale clinic ke paas chale jaate hain jinki website pe photos aur details hoti hain."

**You:** "Aur sabse badi baat Sir, **Google Map unhi Clinics ko upar (Top Rank) dikhata hai** jinki khud ki Website hoti hai. Bina website ke aapka clinic search mein neeche dab jaata hai, bhale hi aapke reviews achhe hon."

---

## 🔵 Phase 3: The Solution (Dawayi)
*Present CGP360 as the cure. Focus on "One-Time".*

**You:** "Isiliye humne **CGP360** start kiya hai specialized sirf Doctors ke liye. Market mein **baaki agencies aur freelancers aapse website banane ka ₹25,000 se ₹30,000 charge karte hain** aur uske baad har mahine Rent (kiraya) leti hain. Hum aapko aapki Digital Property ka **Maalik (Owner)** banate hain."

**You:** "Hum aapki **5-10 Page ki Professional Website** bana ke denge jisme:
1.  Aapki aur Clinic ki **Professional Photos** aur Videos hongi.
2.  Treatment ki list aur prices honge.
3.  Patient ka trust badhane ke liye **Google Map** aur **WhatsApp Button** hoga."

---

## 🟣 Phase 4: The Offer (The "No-Brainer")
*Use the price anchor. Price reveal.*

**You:** "Aur Doctor, iski pricing humne bohot simple rakhi hai."

**You:** "Sirf **₹6,999 (One-Time)** mein hum aapko poora setup karke denge, with **1 Year Hosting & Maintenance FREE**. 
**NOTE:** Isme pure saal chote-mote updates (Jaise photo badalna, text change karna) bilkul FREE included hai. Uske baad life-time wo website aapki hai."

---

## 🔴 Phase 5: The Close (Call to Action)
*Don't ask for money yet. Ask for a chance to show PROOF.*

**You:** "Sir, main jaanta hoon aap busy hain. Main aapse abhi koi commitment nahi chahta."

**You:** "Kya main aapko WhatsApp par **kuch designs (Demos)** bhej sakta hoon jo humne doosre doctors ke liye banaye hain? Aap bas unhe dekh lijiye, agar pasand aaye toh hum aage baat karenge."

*(Wait for "Okay, bhejo")*

**You:** "Thank you Doctor! Is number par WhatsApp hai na? Main abhi bhejta hoon. Please save my number as '[Your Name] - Website Guy'. Have a great day!"

# 🛡️ Objection Handling (Cheat Sheet)

### 1. "Mere paas Justdial / Practo hai, mujhe website nahi chahiye."
**Answer:** "Sir, Justdial ek directory hai jahan **aapke competitor** bhi list hote hain. Wahan patient compare karta hai. **Website aapki khud ki dukan hai**, wahan sirf AAP ho. Aur website ka link aap Justdial pe daloge toh wahan se lead conversion double ho jayega."

### 2. "Bohot mehenga hai (Too Expensive)."
**Answer:** "Sir, ₹6,999 one-time hai. Agar is website se pure saal mein **sirf 2 naye patient** bhi aa gaye, toh aapka paisa wapas (ROI Recovered). Uske baad jo bhi patient aayega wo profit hi hai. Kya aapko nahi lagta 2 patient toh aaram se aa jayenge?"

### 3. "Abhi time nahi hai / Baad mein sochenge."
**Answer:** "Bilkul sir. Main bas chahta hoon aap ek baar **Demo** dekh lijiye taaki aapko idea ho ki modernized clinic kaisa dikhta hai. Dekhne ka koi charge nahi hai. Main WhatsApp kar doon?"

### 4. "Mujhe tech (computer) ka knowledge nahi hai."
**Answer:** "Sir aapko kuch nahi karna. Photo aur text hum aapse WhatsApp pe mangayenge, aur humari team sab setup karegi. Pure saal agar aapko koi photo change karni hai ya price badalna hai, toh wo **Minor Changes bhi isme FREE included hain**. Hum aapko **'Clinic Growth Partner'** banke support karenge."

# 🧠 Strategy Breakdown (Why this works)

### 1. The "Pattern Interrupt" (Opening)
Calling me "Sir" and asking "Loan chahiye?" makes me hang up.
But saying **"Maine Google Map par aapka clinic dekha"** creates curiosity.
*   *Why?* The doctor thinks: "Oh? Did he see a bad review? Did he see something wrong?" **He listens.**

### 2. Digital Real Estate vs Rent
Indinas love **Ownership (Maalik banna)** and hate **Rent (Kiraya dena)**.
Baaki companies "Monthly Fee" mangti hain. You are offering **Lifetime Ownership** for a small one-time fee. This is your biggest selling point.

### 3. Competitor FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out)
Doctors are competitive. Saying **"Pados wala clinic le jayega patient"** hits their ego and fear. They want to be #1 in their area.

# 💡 Pro Tips for Success

1.  **Don't Sell on Call, Sell the Demo**: Your only goal on the call is to get permission to send a WhatsApp message. Once they see the beautiful design, half the sale is done.
2.  **Smile While Talking**: Patients and doctors can "hear" a smile. It makes you sound friendly, not desperate.
3.  **The "Consultant" Tone**: Don't sound beggary ("Sir lelo na"). Sound like an expert ("Sir, ye aapke bhale ke liye hai").
4.  **Follow-up is Key**: 60% of sales happen after the 2nd or 3rd message. Don't give up if they don't reply immediately.

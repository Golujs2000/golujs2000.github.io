FAQ

No. Our packages are one-time. Hosting + maintenance are free for 1 year. Domain is separate.

The clinic owner purchases the domain (usually ₹800–₹1,200/year). We connect it free of cost.

What is included in maintenance?

Minor text/image changes, contact updates, and technical support for your hosted site. Major redesign/layout changes are separate.

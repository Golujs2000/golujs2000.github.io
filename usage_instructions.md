# 📖 How to Use the NotebookLM Audit Prompt

Follow these steps to generate a professional clinic audit in seconds:

### 1. Gather Clinic Data
- Go to Google Maps and search for the clinic.
- Copy the entire "About", "Reviews", and "Overview" sections. 
- (Optional) Copy a few reviews to give the AI more context on patient sentiment.

### 2. Set Up NotebookLM
- Go to [NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google.com/).
- Create a **New Notebook** named after the clinic (e.g., "Audit - Dr. Sharma Dental").
- Click **Add Source** -> **Paste Text**.
- Paste all the clinic information you collected in Step 1.

### 3. Generate the Audit
- Copy the entire content of [notebooklm_prompt.md](file:///c:/Users/hp/Desktop/CGP360/notebooklm_prompt.md).
- Paste it into the NotebookLM chat box.
- Hit Enter.

### 4. Review & Export
- The AI will generate a formatted report.
- Check the **Google Maps Ranking** and **Competitive Analysis** section for accuracy.
- Copy the response into a Markdown editor or a Google Doc to share with the doctor.

---
> [!TIP]
> If the AI misses a specific competitor, you can simply type: *"Please add [Competitor Name] to the comparison table, they have 4.5 stars."*

Website Content Checklist

Please provide the following details to help us build your professional clinic website faster.

1. Clinic Details

  • Official Clinic Name

    Exactly as it should appear on the website.

  • Full Address + Google Map Link

    So patients can find you easily.

  • Contact Number & Email

    For appointments and inquiries.

  • Clinic Timings

    Opening/Closing hours for all days.

2. Doctor Profiles

  • Doctor Name & Qualifications

    e.g., Dr. Rajesh Kumar, MBBS, MD (Dermatology)

  • Short Bio / Introduction

    Years of experience, specializations, university (optional).

  • High-Quality Headshot Photo

    Professional photo of the doctor (Very Important).

3. Services & Photos

  • List of Treatments/Services

    e.g., Root Canal, Acne Treatment, Full Body Checkup.

  • Clinic Photos (Interior & Exterior)

    Reception, Waiting Area, Treatment Room, Building Front.

  • Logo File (If available)

    In PNG or PDF format.

4. Key Achievements (Optional)

  • Patient Count & Success Stats

    e.g., "5000+ Happy Patients", "10+ Years Experience", "98% Success Rate".

  • Awards or Recognitions

    Any gold medals, city awards, or certificates.

5. Domain Name (Website Address)

I have already purchased my domain (e.g., myclinic.com)
I need to buy one (We will guide you on how to buy from GoDaddy)

# 📱 CGP Clinic Growth Partner - WhatsApp Templates (Hindi/Hinglish)

Yahan alag-alag situations ke liye 3 best message formats hain. Aap situation ke hisaab se use kar sakte hain.

---

## 1️⃣ Format 1: Call ke baad (Report Bhejte Waqt)
*Iska use tab karein jab doctor se baat ho chuki hai aur unhone report bhejne ko kaha hai.*

**Template:**

"Namaste Dr. [Name] 🙏,

Abhi aapse call par baat hui thi. Dhanyavaad.

Jaisa maine promise kiya tha, ye rahi aapke clinic ki **Audit Report (PDF)** 📄.

**Is report mein aap dekhenge:**
1. ✅ Kyun [Competitor Name] Google par upar rank kar raha hai.
2. ✅ Aapke clinic ki current online position.
3. ✅ Kaise hum **3 Din** mein aapko Top 3 mein la sakte hain.

Please isse check karein. Kaafi important insights hain.

Regards,
**[Your Name]**
CGP Clinic Growth Partner"

---

## 2️⃣ Format 2: Direct Message (Bina Call Kiye / Cold Message)
*Iska use tab karein jab doctor call nahi utha rahe, par aap unka dhyaan khichna chahte hain.*

**Template:**

"Hello Dr. [Name] 👋,

Maine notice kiya ki Google par **'[City] mein Best [Specialty]'** search karne par aapka clinic top par show nahi ho raha.

Aapke patients shayad competitors ke paas ja rahe hain kyunki unki digital profile optimized hai.

Maine aapke clinic ke liye ek **'Growth Audit Report'** banayi hai jo dikhati hai ki exactly kahan kami hai.

Kya main wo report yahan share kar doon? (Free hai).

Regards,
**[Your Name]**
CGP Clinic Growth Partner"

---

## 3️⃣ Format 3: Follow-up (Agar reply na aaye)
*Iska use report bhejne ke 24 ghante baad karein agar koi jawab na aaye.*

**Template:**

"Namaste Dr. [Name],

Kya aapko Audit Report dekhne ka samay mila?

Report mein jo **'Missing Website'** wala point hai, wo sabse bada reason hai ranking drop hone ka.

Humara **One-Time Setup** package (No Monthly Rent) isse sirf 3 din mein fix kar sakta hai.

Kya hum ek choti call arrange kar sakte hain samjhane ke liye?

Regards,
**[Your Name]**"

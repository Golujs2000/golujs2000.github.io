# 📞 CGP Clinic Growth Partner - Step-by-Step Pitch Script

Ye script ek logical flow mein design ki gayi hai. Bas in steps ko follow karein.

---

## � Step 1: Introduction (Opening)
*Maqsad: Doctor ka dhyaan khichna aur trust banana.*

**You:** "Namaste Dr. [Name]."

**Doctor:** "Namaste, kaun?"

**You:** "Sir/Ma'am, main **[Your Name]** baat kar raha hoon **CGP Clinic Growth Partner** se.

Hum sirf doctors ke saath kaam karte hain unki **Google Ranking** aur **Patients** badhane mein."

---

## 🟠 Step 2: The Hook (Problem Batana)
*Maqsad: Unhe ehsaas dilana ki wo kuch miss kar rahe hain.*

**You:** "Sir, maine abhi notice kiya ki jab hum **'[Specialty] in [City]'** search kar rahe hain, toh **Dr. [Competitor Name]** ka naam sabse upar aa raha hai, lekin aapka clinic neeche show ho raha hai.

Iska matlab hai ki jo naye patients Google par search kar rahe hain, wo directly unke paas ja rahe hain."

---

## 🔵 Step 3: The Solution (Audit Report)
*Maqsad: Unhe solution offer karna (bina kuch beche).*

**You:** "Maine aapke clinic ke liye technically ek **'Audit Report'** banayi hai.

Is report mein maine 3 cheezein highlight ki hain:
1. Aap competitors se peeche kyun hain.
2. Aap mahine mein kitne patients loose kar rahe hain.
3. Aur kaise hum **3 Din** mein aapko top par la sakte hain."

---

## 🟣 Step 4: The Ask (Permission Lena)
*Maqsad: Report bhejne ki permission lena (Call close karna).*

**You:** "Kya main ye Free Audit Report aapko **WhatsApp** par bhej doon? Aap bas ek baar dekh lijiye, kaafi insights milenge."

**Doctor:** "Haan bhej do."

**You:** "Ji shukriya. Main abhi send karta hoon. Please mera number save kar lijiye taaki link open ho jaye."

---

## 🔴 Step 5: Post-Call Action (WhatsApp message)
*Maqsad: Report deliver karna aur conversation start karna.*

**Message Template:**

"Namaste Dr. [Name] 🙏,

Baat karne ke liye dhanyavaad.

📊 **Aapke Clinic ki Audit Report attach hai.**

Isme humne analyse kiya hai:
1. Aapki Google Ranking vs Competitors
2. **Missed Opportunity:** ~50 Naye Patients/Month
3. **Solution:** One-Time Professional Setup (No Monthly Rent)

Report check karke bataiyega. Agar humein Top 3 mein aana hai, toh ye fix karna zaroori hai.

Regards,
**[Your Name]**
CGP Clinic Growth Partner"

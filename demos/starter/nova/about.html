Dr. Sarah Mitchell

BDS, MDS • Chief Dentist

Dr. Sarah Mitchell founded Nova Dental Care with a single mission: to make world-class dentistry accessible and anxiety-free. With over 15 years of clinical experience, she specializes in cosmetic makeovers and complex restorative procedures.

"We believe that a healthy smile is the gateway to confidence. Our clinic combines cutting-edge technology such as 3D scanning and laser dentistry with a warm, human touch to ensure you feel at home."

A Gold Medalist from Manipal College of Dental Sciences, Dr. Mitchell constantly upgrades her skills through international fellowships in Germany and the USA.

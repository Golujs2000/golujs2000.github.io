HydraFacial Elite

The HydraFacial is a non-invastive treatment that detoxifies, rejuvenates, and protects your skin for immediate results with no downtime. It removes dead skin cells and extracts impurities while simultaneously bathing the new skin with cleansing, hydrating, and moisturizing serums.

Ideal For

  • • Dull & Dehydrated Skin
  • • Clogged Pores
  • • Instant Glow (Pre-event)

Procedure

  • • Cleanse & Peel
  • • Extract & Hydrate
  • • Fuse & Protect
60 Mins No Downtime

Advanced Chemical Peels

We use medical-grade peels (Glycolic, Salicylic, TCP) customized to your skin sensitivity. A chemical solution is applied to the skin that causes it to exfoliate and eventually peel off, revealing fresh, new skin that is smoother and less wrinkled.

Target Concerns

  • • Acne & Acne Scars
  • • Pigmentation / Melasma
  • • Fine Lines

Expectations

  • • Mild tingling sensation
  • • 2-3 days of skin shedding
  • • New skin in 5-7 days
30-45 Mins Minimal Peeling

Vampire Facial (PRP)

Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) therapy uses injections of a concentration of a patient's own platelets to accelerate the healing of injured tendons, ligaments, muscles and joints. In aesthetics, we use it to boost collagen and rejuvenate the skin.

Benefits

  • • Improves skin texture
  • • Reduces fine lines
  • • Treats under-eye dark circles

Process

  • • Blood draw (10ml)
  • • Centrifugation
  • • Microneedling / Injection
60 Mins 1 Day Redness

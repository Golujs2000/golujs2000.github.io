World-Class Treatments

We use the latest technology to ensure your treatment is precise, effective, and as comfortable as possible.

General Dentistry

Comprehensive Checkups

Full mouth X-rays, cavity detection, and gum health assessment using intraoral cameras.

Takes: 30-45 mins

Cleaning & Polishing

Professional scaling to remove plaque and tartar buildup, ensuring healthy gums and fresh breath.

Takes: 45 mins

Fillings & Restorations

Tooth-colored composite fillings to repair cavities and restore the natural look of your teeth.

Takes: 30-60 mins

Cosmetic & Orthodontics

Teeth Whitening

Zoom® Laser Whitening for instant results. Up to 8 shades lighter in a single visit.

Takes: 1 hr

Invisalign (Clear Aligners)

Straighten your teeth discreetly without metal braces. Custom-made clear trays.

Duration: 6-12 Months

Veneers & Laminates

Thin porcelain shells to cover chipped, stained, or uneven teeth for a celebrity smile.

Takes: 2-3 Visits

Surgical & Restorative

Root Canal Treatment

Advanced rotary endodontics to save infected teeth. Carried out under local anesthesia.

Dental Implants

Titanium posts surgically placed in the jawbone to replace missing tooth roots.

Wisdom Tooth Extraction

Safe removal of impacted wisdom teeth with minimal downtime and pain management.

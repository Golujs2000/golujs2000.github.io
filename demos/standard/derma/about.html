Dr. Ananya Sharma

MD, Dermatology (Gold Medalist) • Medical Director

Dr. Ananya Sharma is a renowned dermatologist and aesthetic physician with over a decade of experience in transforming skin and hair health. Her journey began at one of India's premier medical institutes, where she not only excelled academically but developed a deep passion for clinical dermatology.

She believes that every skin story is unique. "There is no one-size-fits-all in skincare," she says. "My goal is to listen to your concerns, understand your lifestyle, and craft a treatment plan that delivers real, sustainable results."

After completing her MD from AIIMS New Delhi, she pursued advanced fellowship training in lasers and aesthetic medicine in South Korea, mastering techniques that are minimally invasive yet maximally effective.
